Lists with data-upcoming-list: rows with data-date get .is-upcoming (date today or later). With data-reorder: upcoming rows first (soonest on top), then past rows (newest on top), at most data-max-past past rows shown. data-heading names the id of a heading whose text switches between data-text-upcoming and data-text-past.
