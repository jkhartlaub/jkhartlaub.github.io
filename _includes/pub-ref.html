{%- comment -%} Looks up a publication in _data/refs.json (generated from files/refs.bib by scripts/bib_to_data.py) and sets pub_ref (with .bibtex and, if refs.bib has one, .abstract), or nil. Key: the DOI from `link:` in lower case, otherwise `link:` in lower case without scheme, "www." and trailing slash. Usage: {% include pub-ref.html pub=page %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign pr = include.pub -%} {%- if pr.link contains "doi.org/" -%} {%- assign pub_ref_key = pr.link | split: "doi.org/" | last | downcase -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign pub_ref_key = pr.link | downcase | remove_first: "https://" | remove_first: "http://" | remove_first: "www." | append: "#" | remove: "/#" | remove: "#" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign pub_ref = site.data.refs[pub_ref_key] -%}
