Imagine you live in an office building with some number of floors, each of which is infinitely large.

You want to calculate the total influence of all of your co-workers on you, where the farther away they are, the weaker the influence is (this is the situation shown in figure (b) in the post below).

Interestingly, this would be way easier to calculate if the building were infinitely tall as well, as opposed to some fixed number of floors!

This type of problem appears in the calculation of forces in a micro-magnetic film, which can be understood as being very thin but close to infinitely large.
