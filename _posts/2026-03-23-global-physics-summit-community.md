For the second and last time this week, I passed by a giant blue horse 🐴 with menacing red eyes, the iconic yet controversial landmark just outside Denver airport. In contrast with this frightening mammal made of glass, fittingly nicknamed 'Bluecifer', this year's Global Physics Summit [American Physical Society](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aps-physics/) in Downtown Denver proved to be the most welcoming space, filled to the brim with physicists from all over the world 🌍 exchanging the latest results, many of which related to the Quantum Computing Ecosystem ⚛️.

*Global Physics Summit, Part II — Community*

I am elated to have taken part in this (thanks to the [Deutsches Zentrum für Luft-und Raumfahrt e.V.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dlr/)) together with my colleague [Joe Harris](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joe-harris-608107159/); being able to share my research with a global audience (see Part I of this post!) and connect with the community.

My goal doing applied research is ultimately to develop methods and algorithms for the community to accept and adopt them, so they will ultimately be useful to industry. Therefore, one of my favorite places at the convention center was again the exhibitor hall, where one could connect with the quantum industry and chat with experts building actual devices, even from fields completely unrelated to my own research 🏭. There, one could for example get a hands-on introduction to research-grade vacuum pumps at different pressure scales by physics-savvy exhibitors at the [Edwards Vacuum](https://www.linkedin.com/company/edwards/) booth 🔬.

Though my favorite booth was (again) the [Qblox](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qblox/) booth, which featured talks by industry experts, a fun post-it question wall 📝, food, hardware demonstrators, and hands down the best merchandise in the hall. I already feel like a (completely unofficial) brand ambassador, wearing my QBlox 🟦 bag, hat, stickers, and matching earrings; just missing some QBlox trousers 👖 (to give some next-year merchandise inspiration to [Julia Muñoz Navarro](https://www.linkedin.com/in/juliamunoznavarro/) who also organized the exceptionally well-planned quantum tech community gathering 🎉).
