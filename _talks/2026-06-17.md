Here we provide a broad overview of methods and algorithms based on Epstein zeta functions and their generalizations, such as many-body zeta functions, how to include non-translationally invariant gemoetries and representations for its wave-vector derivatives.
Their applications to the physics of long-range interacting matter is shown, in particular in micromagnetism and theoretical chemistry.
